🛡️ About CivicTrack-AI

Civic Infrastructure & Intelligent Road Monitoring Platform

CivicTrack-AI is an intelligent civic road infrastructure and smart travel safety platform designed to empower citizens to report road hazards and assist commuters in planning safer journeys.

Using Computer Vision & NLP, incoming reports are instantly evaluated for structural severity, classified into High, Medium, or Low priority, and routed to road authorities for transparent resolution.

Key Pillars:

  • Automated Severity Engine: Evaluates road craters, waterlogging & obstacles with confidence scores.
  • Safe Journey Routing: Live hazard buffer calculations, toll estimates & traffic risk indexing.
  • Authority Tracking: Closed-loop governance with timestamped municipal response notes.
